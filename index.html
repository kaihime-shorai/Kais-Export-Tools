What it does

One click in the popup, one file on your computer.

💬

Export everything

  • Full chat history as a transcript
  • Knowledge base tabs
  • Long-term memories
  • Training pairs
  • Profile & configuration
📖

Read it back beautifully

Story Reader turns transcripts into properly typeset stories. Data Viewer opens any export with full Markdown — themes, brightness control, font scaling, focus mode and search included. Every setting is remembered.

🔒

Private by design

No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no tracking. Everything runs on your machine and files save straight to your device. The extension only touches shapes.inc, and only when you click a button.
